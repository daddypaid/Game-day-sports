GameDay Privacy Notice

How this test app uses account and game information

HomeAccountSettingsHelp & FAQ

TEST MODE — no real money. This app uses test credits and does not accept real-money deposits or provide cash withdrawals.

Information used by the app

When you create an account, GameDay’s authentication service processes your email address and password. The app uses your account identifier to retrieve your test wallet and your records.

The backend records test wallet transactions, sportsbook tickets, game actions and casino results so accepted wagers can be settled and interrupted requests can be recovered.

Information you type into a support email is shared with the support recipient when you choose to send it.

Information saved in this browser

The app saves your sign-in session, game sound preferences and identifiers for unfinished game or wager requests in browser storage. These request identifiers help recover a result without charging your test wallet twice.

Sign out when you finish on a shared device. If a wager or spin is still recovering, resolve it before clearing site data or changing devices.

Services the app connects to

GameDay’s website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Authentication, account data and game services use Supabase. Some browser libraries load from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to these services when it loads or uses the app.

This notice describes the current test app. For questions about your account information, contact the GameDay support recipient through Help.

Questions about your information

Use Help & FAQ for support and privacy questions. Never include your password, a password-reset link or a sign-in token in a message.
