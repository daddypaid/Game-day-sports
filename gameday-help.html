Frequently asked questions

I cannot connect or sign in

Check your connection and use Retry or Refresh on the page. If your session has expired, open Account and sign in again. Avoid repeated wager submissions while a request is recovering.

My password reset link has expired

Open Account and request a new password-reset email. Use the most recent link. Do not share reset links.

A wager or spin is still recovering

Use the page’s Recover or Retry control to check the original request. Keep the same account signed in. A recovered receipt shows the accepted result; do not clear site data while that request is unresolved.

Where can I find my results?

My Bets contains sportsbook tickets. Casino History contains recorded slot grids and settled table results, stakes and payouts. Account contains test-wallet transactions. Earlier results that predate casino receipts may only have wallet transactions.

Why can I not refill my test wallet?

Refills follow the service’s balance and cooldown rules. Account shows when you are eligible again. A refill adds test credits only.

Why is a market view-only or a wager rejected?

Some sports and markets cannot be graded automatically and are view-only. Odds may change or a selection may become suspended before placement. Read the rejection message, refresh the lines and review the selections before trying again. Same-event selections cannot be combined into a parlay.

How do I turn off game sounds or animations?

Open Settings to change game sound preferences. GameDay follows your device’s reduced motion accessibility setting.
